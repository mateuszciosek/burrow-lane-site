This policy covers Fire Station Crew, Baby Dragons and Ice Cream Parlour, published by Mateusz Ciosek under Burrow Lane, Wishwood and Little Makers. The games are for children aged 2–5; purchases, settings and external links are behind a parental gate.

Children's play and local storage

No app account or sign-in is required. We do not ask children for names, ages, photos, recordings, contacts or precise location. There are no ads, child profiling or behavioural advertising. Progress, stamps, settings, the play timer and a small play log stay on the device and are not uploaded to us. Purchase confirmations are also stored locally.

Live catalogue and hosting

Gameplay works offline. When connected, the app automatically refreshes its public programme catalogue about once daily and may download artwork using HTTPS from Google's Firebase Hosting. Requests do not include a child profile, play history, saved progress or an app-generated user identifier. Hosting nevertheless receives IP addresses and normal technical connection information. Firebase says Hosting uses IPs for abuse detection and usage analysis and retains IP data for a few months. This is not anonymous or merely momentary processing. Google may process hosting data on its global infrastructure.

Google Play and the Pass

Google Play handles payments, purchase history and payment-service technical processing under its own terms and privacy policy. The app reads product prices and purchase status; we do not receive card details. Google Play and its billing components may process technical diagnostics about payments. Our compatible apps on the same device can locally share an active Pass status and confirmation time, not a child's play history. There is no Burrow Lane cloud account or cross-device family entitlement service.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process visitor IPs and request metadata under GitHub's privacy statement. We add no advertising, analytics cookies or external font services. If a parent emails us, we receive their address and message to answer and resolve support or purchase requests. Please do not send identifiable information about children. Messages are retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Deletion, rights and security

Delete local saves in the parent corner, clear Android app storage or uninstall. This does not delete Google Play purchase records or automatically remove hosting logs. Contact us about access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights; we will explain what we control and how to contact the relevant provider. Where data protection law applies, necessary operation/security processing relies on legitimate interests, and support/purchase requests on service delivery and legitimate interests. You may complain to your local data protection authority. Catalogue traffic uses HTTPS; absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes and contact
