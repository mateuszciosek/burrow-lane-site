Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

This policy covers the Burrow Lane games for children on Android: Fire Station Crew, Baby Dragons and Ice Cream Parlour, and later Burrow Lane games that link to it. They are published by Mateusz Ciosek ("we", "us"), who is responsible for them.

The short version

  • We collect nothing about your child, or about you. No name, no age, no photos, no voice, no location, no contacts, no device identifiers, no advertising ID.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking. The games contain no advertising, analytics or tracking code from anyone.
  • Progress stays on the device: stamps, unlocked levels and settings are saved only on the phone or tablet. We cannot see them.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or account details.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.

What stays on the device

To keep your child's place, each game saves on the device: which episodes and games were played, stamps earned, the level reached in the Playroom, the play timer's setting and today's play time, the chosen language and sound settings, and which purchases this device owns. A small play log (episodes started and finished, times the grown-ups' question was shown) also stays on the device for the parent corner's progress card. None of this is sent anywhere.

Purchases and the Burrow Lane Pass

A season or the Pass is bought through Google Play, behind a question only a grown-up can answer. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy and tells the game on the device that the purchase is valid. When the Pass is bought in one Burrow Lane game, our other Burrow Lane games on the same device can see that it is active, through Android itself, on the device: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Internet

The games play offline. They only talk to the Google Play app on the device, to show prices and confirm purchases. Links to the store, this site or e-mail open only after the grown-ups' question.

Children's privacy

The games are made for children aged 2 to 5 and are designed so that no personal information is collected from them, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing we can share, sell or disclose.

Deleting saved data

Use "Delete saved data" in the parent corner, clear the game's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the game. Purchases stay with your Google account and can be restored with "Restore purchases".

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date, and a game that started to collect anything would ask a grown-up first. Questions: support@burrowlane.app.
