These terms cover Fire Station Crew, Baby Dragons, Ice Cream Parlour and later Burrow Lane games, published by Mateusz Ciosek. They sit alongside Google Play's terms, which govern how you buy and pay.

What is free

The first five episodes of every game, and the Playroom games those episodes introduce, are free for ever.

Season purchase

One purchase in a game opens that game's whole season of episodes and every game in its Playroom. It is a one-time purchase: no renewal. It stays with your Google account and can be restored on another device with "Restore purchases".

Burrow Lane Pass

  • The Pass opens every Burrow Lane game, including new ones released while it is active.
  • It starts with a free trial when one is offered. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it becomes a paid yearly subscription at the price shown in Google Play.
  • It renews every year until you cancel. Cancel any time in Google Play: Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. You keep the Pass until the end of the period already paid.
  • The Pass bought in one game also opens our other games installed on the same device with the same Google account.

Refunds

Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy, and your consumer rights where you live are not affected. Write to us if something went wrong.
