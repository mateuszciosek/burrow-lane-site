These terms cover the three Burrow Lane apps alongside Google Play's purchase terms. Only grown-ups should purchase. The first five episodes and their Playroom games are available without purchase. A one-time season purchase unlocks that app's season and Playroom, not the other apps.

Polish launch prices: 9.99 PLN per app during the initial promotion, standard price 14.99 PLN; annual auto-renewing Pass 39.99 PLN, with no free trial. Prices/currencies vary by territory; the exact price, billing period and renewal terms shown by Google Play before confirmation govern the purchase.

The Pass covers all current and future Burrow Lane apps as they are released while the Pass is active. An active Pass unlocks compatible apps installed on the same device. Restore in the original purchasing app first, then open the siblings. It is not a cloud family account or automatic sharing across devices. On another device, first install and restore the purchasing app with the same Google account. Do not buy another Pass just because a sibling has not refreshed.

Offline confirmations are cached for up to 35 days after the most recent confirmation; this does not extend the paid subscription period. Cancel renewal in Google Play: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pass. Access normally continues through the paid period. Local offline confirmations may remain cached temporarily; they are not another paid period. Refunds follow Google Play's policy and applicable consumer rights. Contact us about purchase or restoration problems.
