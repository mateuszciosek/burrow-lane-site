Burrow Lane

Calm games for children aged 2 to 5, played like a TV show

Every Burrow Lane game is a little series. Budi, a seed sprite, pops out of his burrow and opens each episode; then your child helps the cast through two or three gentle mini-games, earns a stamp for the sticker book and hears what happens next time. Nothing to read, no timers, nothing to lose: a wrong move simply glides back.

Fire Station Crew

Rescue kittens, wash Big Red, stop the traffic for the ducklings.

Baby Dragons

Hatch a baby dragon in Wishwood, feed the little ones, climb the clouds.

Ice Cream Parlour

Scoop, swirl and serve in the seal family's seaside shop.

For parents

  • No ads, and child play records stay on the device. The live catalogue uses hosting; see our privacy policy. No accounts, no sign-in.
  • The first five episodes of every game are free for ever. The rest of a season is one purchase, or the Burrow Lane Pass opens every game.
  • Purchases, settings and links sit behind a question for grown-ups.
  • A play timer you set ends the session calmly, with a goodnight scene.
  • Gameplay works offline. The public catalogue refreshes automatically when connected.
