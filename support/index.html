Where are the settings?

Tap the gear on the first screen and answer the grown-ups' question. The parent corner has the play timer, language, subtitles, sound, your child's progress, purchases and "Delete saved data".

I bought it, but episodes are still locked

In the parent corner, tap "Restore purchases". Make sure the device uses the same Google account that made the purchase. For the Burrow Lane Pass, open the game where you bought it once, so the other games on the device can see it.

How do I cancel the Pass?

In Google Play: your profile picture, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, Burrow Lane Pass, Cancel. The parent corner's "Manage subscription" goes straight there.

The timer ended the game

That is the play timer you set. When time is up the current episode finishes and the game says goodnight. A grown-up can wake it, or change the timer in the parent corner. The time counts per day.

Does it need the internet?

No. Only buying and restoring purchases talk to Google Play.
